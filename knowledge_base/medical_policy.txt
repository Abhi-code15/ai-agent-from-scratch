Medical Policy - Group Health Insurance
Zorvex Technologies provides comprehensive group health insurance coverage of up to 500,000 rupees per year for all full-time employees.

Medical Policy - Dependent Coverage
The insurance policy covers the employee, legally married spouse, and up to two dependent children up to the age of 25.

Medical Policy - Cashless Hospitalization
Cashless admission is supported across 6,500 network hospitals nationwide using the digital health card accessible on the Zorvex Benefits app.

Medical Policy - Maternity Cover
The policy covers maternity expenses up to 50,000 rupees for normal delivery and 75,000 rupees for caesarean section. Pre-hospitalization and post-hospitalization medical bills are covered for 30 and 60 days respectively.
