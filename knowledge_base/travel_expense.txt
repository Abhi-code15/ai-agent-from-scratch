Travel Expense Policy - Pre-Approval Requirement
All business travel must be formally pre-approved by the department head at least 7 business days prior to departure.

Travel Expense Policy - Domestic Travel Entitlements
Employees traveling domestically are eligible for economy airfare for journeys exceeding 4 hours. Hotel accommodations are capped at 4,000 rupees per night in Tier 1 cities.

Travel Expense Policy - Per Diem Allowance
A daily per diem allowance of 1,200 rupees is granted for meals and local transit. Receipts are not required for per diem claims under the threshold.

Travel Expense Policy - Expense Claim Settlement
Expense reimbursement claims with original GST invoices must be submitted via the Zorvex Finance portal within 15 calendar days of trip conclusion.
