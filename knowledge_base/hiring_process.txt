Hiring Process - Interview Stages
The interview process at Zorvex Technologies consists of five distinct rounds: initial HR screening, technical round 1, technical round 2, managerial round, and HR round.

Hiring Process - Offer Acceptance
After the final round, offer letters are issued within 3 business days. A candidate has 7 days to accept the offer before it expires.

Hiring Process - Employee Referral Bonus
Employees who refer successful candidates receive an employee referral bonus of 25,000 rupees, paid after the new joiner completes 90 days at Zorvex.

Hiring Process - Pre-Onboarding
HR sends the onboarding checklist and IT asset requisition form two days before the joining date.
