HR Documentation - Working Hours and Attendance
Standard working hours at Zorvex Technologies are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday. A grace period of 15 minutes is allowed for morning check-in.

HR Documentation - Hybrid Work Policy
Employees in eligible teams can work remotely up to two days per week with prior manager approval. Core team meeting days require mandatory in-office presence.

HR Documentation - Workplace Code of Conduct
Zorvex maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against workplace harassment, bullying, or discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or background.

HR Documentation - Performance Appraisal Cycles
Employee performance reviews occur bi-annually in June and December. Appraisals directly determine annual merit increments, bonuses, and promotion tracks.
